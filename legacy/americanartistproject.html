Act II

Amplify Austin Campaign

Amplify Austin Day is a 24‑hour, community‑wide giving event. Each year, Central Texans donate online. Since 2007, the program has generated $139 million for area organizations. It’s a highly competitive environment, with charities vying for donations and recognition.

Challenge

No budget, bigger competition

Boost awareness and attendance without paid media, against organizations several times AAP's size.

Insight

Recognition drives participation

Partners and micro-influencers support an event more when recognized as contributors, not an audience.

Strategy

Share the spotlight

Researched organizations and micro-influencers whose values aligned with AAP's mission. From there, I wrote tailored emails inviting them to become part of the event, which led to several partners sharing the experience on their Instagram Stories.

Broadway Brunch — Portland, Maine Curation

Broadway Brunch is AAP's monthly donor event, where grant recipients perform and share their work with supporters. This edition brought donors together for a curated weekend in Portland, Maine. I independently researched, wrote, and designed the guide as an extension of the event, organizing local recommendations into easy to navigate sections so guests could spend less time planning and more time connecting with the city, the community, and the arts.
