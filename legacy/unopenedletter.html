Nicholas Jimenez

Pianist — Composer — Educator

Nicholas Jimenez, M.M. is a renowned American concert pianist, educator, and composer based in Austin, Texas. Known for blending virtuosity and innovative musicality, Nicholas has performed across four continents and won prizes in numerous prestigious piano competitions. As former roommates, we decided to collaborate on a music video to promote his new piece. For this campaign, we wanted to attach a distinct feeling to his song, so we focused on the universal ache of Longing.

Copywriting & Testing

Final Ad Copy

"A NEW PIANO SOUND FOR THOSE DAYS THAT FEEL LIKE MEMORIES"

The main focus of our messaging was to position the track as a cinematic soundtrack for the listener's internal life. To find the right hook, we A/B tested "A New Piano Sound" against "The Definitive Soundtrack." We chose to move forward with "A New Piano Sound" due to its performance compared to the alternative. Our ultimate hope is that this hook encourages listeners to tie their own memories to the song so they will always return to it.

Video Campaign

A Story Preserved in Time

The video campaign focused on the bittersweet feeling of longing for moments that have passed. We aimed to create a visual parallel to the memories the music evokes.
